The Process

How It Works

We handle the complexity so you don't have to. From first call to closing, we're with you every step of the way.

1

Free Consultation

We review your situation: your mortgage balance, property value, and timeline, with zero cost and zero obligation. You'll know your options before making any decision.

2

Your Hardship Package

Once you decide to go ahead, we gather your documents and let your lender know you are pursuing a short sale. Missing paperwork is the most common reason lenders push back, so we make sure nothing is left out.

3

Listed & Offer Sent to Your Lender

We list your property on the MLS. When a buyer makes an offer, we send it to your lender together with your full short sale package.

4

Approval & Closing

Your lender approves, counters or declines, typically within 30 to 60 days of your first call. You see the terms before anything is final. At closing, the home is sold on those terms and the situation is behind you. Many lenders also help with moving costs.

All questions

A short sale is when your lender agrees to accept less than the full amount owed on your mortgage so you can sell the home. Whether the lender forgives the rest depends on who owns your loan, and it is written in the approval. It's a legal, lender-approved process that lets homeowners who owe more than their home is worth sell without going through foreclosure.

From your first call, lender approval typically takes 30 to 60 days. Closing after that depends on the buyer and the lender. Our services are paid through the real estate commission at closing, so you pay nothing out of pocket to work with us. The first consultation is free.

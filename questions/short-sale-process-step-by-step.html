What happens in a short sale, step by step?

Short answer

We review your hardship and gather your documents, the home is listed and sold like any other, and the buyer's offer goes to your lender with your short sale package. The lender approves, counters or declines. From your first call to an approved sale typically takes 30 to 60 days. Closing after that varies.

The steps

  1. You contact us. A first conversation about your situation. No cost and no commitment.
  2. Hardship and eligibility review. We ask about your hardship and your loan to see whether a short sale fits.
  3. We gather your documents. Once you decide to go ahead, we take it from there and collect what the lender needs. Missing documents are the most common reason lenders push back, so this step matters.
  4. Your lender is told. We let your lender know you are pursuing a short sale. Some lenders start their review at this point. Others wait until there is an offer.
  5. The home is listed. It goes on the MLS like any other sale.
  6. An offer comes in. A buyer makes an offer, which depends on the lender's approval.
  7. The package goes to the lender. The offer is sent together with all of your short sale documents.
  8. The lender decides. It reviews the documents, confirms the hardship, and approves the sale, counters, or declines.
  9. The approval documents arrive. They set the terms of the sale, including whether the lender waives the difference between the sale price and what you owe.
  10. Closing is scheduled.
  11. Closing. The sale closes on the lender's terms, and the situation is behind you.

How long it takes

From your first call to an approved short sale typically takes 30 to 60 days. Closing after the approval depends on the buyer and the lender, so that part varies.

The biggest thing you can do to keep it moving is to get your documents in early. Missing paperwork is what slows most short sales down.

Will I owe taxes on the debt forgiven in a short sale?

Short answer

It depends on your situation, and we are not the right people to answer it. A tax professional is. Ask before you close, not after.

Why the question comes up

In a short sale, the lender may agree to forgive part of what you owe. Debt that is forgiven can have tax consequences, and whether it does for you depends on details of your situation that only a tax professional can weigh.

Who to ask

A tax professional. Talk to one before you close, not after.

What to bring them

  • The lender's short sale approval letter
  • Your closing statement, once you have it
  • Any tax form the lender sends you about the sale
  • Your recent tax returns

When to ask

Before you close. Knowing the answer ahead of time means no surprises at tax time, and it may affect how you plan for the year.

Can I get help with moving costs in a short sale?

Short answer

Often, yes. Most lenders offer a relocation allowance when they approve a short sale. It can range from nothing up to about $15,000, depending on the lender, and the lender decides the amount case by case. It is not guaranteed.

What it is

A relocation allowance is money a lender puts toward your move as part of the short sale. It exists because a short sale saves the lender the time and cost of a foreclosure.

Most homeowners we talk to have never heard of it. It is usually one of the first things we tell people.

Who decides

Your lender, and only your lender. Whether it is offered, how much, and any conditions attached are part of the lender's approval and are written in the approval documents.

Nobody can promise you an amount at the start. Anyone who does is guessing.

What we do about it

  • We raise it on the first call, so you know it exists before you decide anything.
  • We tell you plainly when we know your lender does not offer it.
  • We go through the approval documents with you so you know exactly what you will and will not receive.
